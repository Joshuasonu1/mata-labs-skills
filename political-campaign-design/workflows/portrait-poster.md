# Workflow 3: Premium portrait poster (from R3)

**For:** introductions, festival greetings, birthdays, "your representative", trust-building.

## Composition
- The face is the hero: head and shoulders filling 55–70% of the frame, eye-line at about 35% from the top, placed left or right third.
- Text stack on the opposite side: two-tier condensed headline plus a small line.
- One badge (ward or constituency number, years of service) in a rounded rectangle; party logo small in a top corner.

## Background
Dark, quiet, atmospheric: dusk skyline, constituency landmark at night, soft bokeh. ChatGPT plate: *"vertical 4:5 moody dusk skyline of a small Indian town, warm brown haze, soft bokeh lights, dark and desaturated, empty right side, no people, no text"*. Add dust particles (generated on black, Screen 40%).

## Colour
Sepia-warm umber plus a gold or party accent; the face naturally warm.

## Typography
Condensed heavy display, white with a clipped grunge texture (Overlay 50%), stepped sizes; an accent "!" or rule in gold.

## Portrait treatment
Needs a **high-res smiling studio photo**; soft key; background 2 stops darker; warm rim on the shadow side (Screen 20%); never smooth skin.

## Lighting
Front-left soft key, warm rim, dark surround.

## Textures
Grunge in the type only; dust particles; grain 30%.

## Photoshop finishing
Gradient map sepia 15%, vignette 30%, slight clarity on the background only (not the face).

## QC focus
Upscaling: the face ≤ 1.5× native. The background must not have bright spots near the head.
