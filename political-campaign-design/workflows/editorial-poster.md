# Workflow 2: Modern editorial typography poster (from R2)

**For:** slogans, "vote for", campaign taglines, youth and urban audiences.

## Composition
- A **giant wordmark** (name or slogan) across the top half, layered **behind** the leader's head.
- Leader waist-up, centred, power pose (arms crossed, hands clasped, confident look).
- 1–2 coverline blocks on the sides (bold line plus 2–3 small lines), date or constituency in the top corners, credit or handle strip at the bottom.
- A foreground band (flags, crowd from real photos, landscape edge) crossing the lower torso.

## Background
Clean sky top plus horizon band (lake, hills, city) plus foreground. ChatGPT plate: *"vertical 4:5 photoreal pale bright sky, calm blue lake and distant hills in the lower third, empty centre, no people, no text"*. Real flags or crowd cut-outs from the client's event photos.

## Colour
Cool whites and blues plus one party accent for the wordmark and flags.

## Typography
Wordmark: heavy flat display, cap height 25–35% of the canvas width, party colour, no effects. Coverlines: bold plus regular pairs. Two families max.

## Portrait treatment
Natural colour, crisp mask, slight cool balance to match the sky; a subtle contact shadow where the flags overlap.

## Lighting
Bright daylight, soft and even; a slight rim from the sky side.

## Textures
Minimal: light grain (15%), maybe subtle paper.

## Photoshop finishing
Layer order: sky, wordmark, landscape, portrait, flags/foreground, coverlines. Keep 30% calm space.

## QC focus
The wordmark must remain readable even with the head overlapping (overlap ≤ 25% of the letters); coverlines ≥ 28px.
