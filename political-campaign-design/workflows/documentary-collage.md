# Workflow 5: Documentary photo-collage poster (from R5)

**For:** works done, schemes delivered, constituency service, event recaps, track record.

## Composition
- A central 3/4 cut-out (smiling, hands together or gesturing).
- **3–5 photo cards** behind or beside: white border, rotation −6° to +6°, soft shadows from one light direction, slight overlaps. **Only the client's real photos** of works and events.
- Behind: a faded wide crowd and a faded landmark (office, district building) at 20–40%, blended into the sky.
- Name as the headline at the bottom (gradient), location or circle above, tagline below.
- Variant used before: a torn-paper editorial band (headline on a cream band plus a yellow brush) with a real crowd strip below and a round colour inset portrait. It works well for reel-cover series.

## Background
Sky (blue to cream haze plus birds), from a ChatGPT plate (*"vertical 3:4 soft blue sky with light clouds fading to warm cream haze, golden hour, a few distant birds, no people, no text"*) or real photo skies.

## Colour
A single golden-hour wash (Photo Filter warming 30% over the whole document) so the photos unify.

## Typography
Name: heavy display with a 2-stop gradient (orange to red, or party); tagline italic/regular with ellipsis; credit tiny.

## Portrait treatment
Warm match to the wash; light rim; contact shadow on the cards behind.

## Lighting
Low golden sun from one side, consistent on the cards and portrait.

## Textures
Polaroid paper, subtle film grain 25%; tape scraps optional.

## Photoshop finishing
Cards as Smart Objects with Stroke (inside, white) plus Drop Shadow; gentle bloom on the highlights.

## QC focus
Each card recognisable at phone size (≥ 22% of the width); no stock or generated photos as evidence; captions or claims only if supplied.
