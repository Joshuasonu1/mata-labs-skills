# Workflow 4: Minimalist white editorial poster (from R4)

**For:** quotes, vision statements, women leaders, policy messages, calm and modern tone.

## Composition
- White or warm-grey canvas, ≥ 40% negative space.
- Full-body or 3/4 leader **walking or gesturing** on a simple graphic path (curved road ribbon) leading toward the quote.
- A giant pale watermark (name) at 5–8%.
- A quote inside a large white quotation-mark shape with a soft shadow; key phrases coloured.
- Name block plus 2–4 credentials (bold label plus text), and a constituency tag top-right.

## Background
A pencil-sketch skyline or landmark at Multiply 30–50%. ChatGPT plate: *"vertical 4:5 minimal pencil line sketch of an Indian town skyline on pure white, light grey lines, mostly empty white space, no people, no text"*. Soft white smoke at the path base.

## Colour
Neutral; pick 1–2 colours **from the leader's clothing** and use them in the key words and name.

## Typography
Quote: mixed weights (key phrases heavy plus gradient gold-orange or brand; the rest regular #333). Name heavy in deep brand colour. Credentials 28–34px.

## Portrait treatment
Natural colour, clean edges, a soft ground shadow at the feet; no rim light needed.

## Lighting
Even, bright, soft.

## Textures
Very light paper grain (10%); no grunge.

## Photoshop finishing
Keep it crisp; no vignette; align everything to a clear grid.

## QC focus
Negative space respected; the quote reads in 3 seconds; credentials legible on a phone.
