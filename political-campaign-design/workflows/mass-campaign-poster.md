# Workflow 6: Mass rally campaign poster (from R6 plus ktrtrs rally covers)

**For:** rallies, roadshows, nominations, poll-day appeals ("vote for symbol / ballot number"), victory celebrations.

## Composition
- Top or left: a **torn cream scroll** or a big title block with the slogan (hero words huge, connector small).
- Hero: a large portrait (speaking at mics, waving, fist raised, namaste), chest-up.
- Bottom 25–35%: a **real rally photo strip** (the client's own crowd footage or photos) fading up, with party flags.
- A diagonal prop (mic stand, flag pole) linking the portrait and text.
- Campaign info: ballot number and symbol (client files), date, constituency in a strong badge.

## Background
Party-colour gradient plus sun flare plus faint ornament (mandala, rangoli) at 8–15%, or a real rally frame blurred and graded. ChatGPT plate: *"vertical 9:16 warm golden-yellow abstract background with a soft sun flare top left and faint traditional ornament pattern, no people, no text"*.

## Colour
Saturated party palette (e.g. TDP yellow-dominant plus red accent); warm, high energy.

## Typography
Hero headline heavy display with texture fill plus subtle bevel or solid extrusion; a ballot number as a giant numeral (ktrtrs style "0", "10/10"); outline or label bar for text over the crowd.

## Portrait treatment
Warm match; strong rim on the text-facing edge; foreground crowd heads or flags overlapping the bottom for depth.

## Lighting
Bright warm sun from the headline side; the crowd strip slightly darker.

## Textures
Torn scroll with fibres and a curl shadow; grain 30%; light dust.

## Photoshop finishing
Selective colour to push the party colour; vignette away from the headline; check that the crowd has no rival flags or symbols in view.

## QC focus
Crowd is real and the client's own; the ballot number and symbol are correct; the slogan exact; energetic but not cluttered (≤ 3 text blocks).
