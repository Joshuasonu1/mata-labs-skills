# Workflow 1: Dramatic cinematic poster (from R1)

**For:** historic decisions, victories, strong statements, confronting an issue.

## Composition (9:16 / 4:5)
- Top 35–45%: a flat dark type panel. Bottom 55–65%: the scene plus portrait. Divided by **one** torn edge (cream rim, soft shadow).
- Portrait: chest-up, mid-gesture (pointing, fist, speaking at mics), cropped by the bottom edge; face in the grid-safe band (y 285–1635 on 9:16).
- **Ghost layer** behind the portrait: symbol (lion, landmark, monument, or a larger faded image of the same leader from another real photo) at 15–35%, Screen.

## Background
- Panel: #2B1509 with paper grain.
- Scene: ChatGPT plate *"vertical 9:16 dark cinematic background, intense orange-golden fire glow and embers rising, smoky atmosphere, no people, no text, no objects"*, plus a radial glow centred behind the head.

## Colour
Warm monochrome (chocolate, orange, amber, peach).

## Typography
Interlocked lockup: the hero word or number at 220–320px, connectors at 70–110px beside it; peach #FFD8B0 with solid extrusion (#C2541A, distance 12, size 0); one word white; the last line on a cream label bar (dark text).

## Portrait treatment
Curves to match the warm plate; orange rim light on the hair and shoulder edge facing the glow (Screen 25%); no dark halo.

## Lighting
Backlight from behind and above; key from the front; low-key.

## Textures
Torn edge (fibrous), grain 30%, light smoke over the lower body.

## Photoshop finishing
Gradient map warm 15%, S-curve, vignette 25%, grain. Check the face is the brightest area.

## QC focus
Don't over-glow; the ghost layer must not compete with the face; the headline readable at thumbnail size.
