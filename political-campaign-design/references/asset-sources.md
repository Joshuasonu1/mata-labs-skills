## Rules
- **Political use:** some stock licences restrict use in political endorsements or with sensitive subjects (Adobe Stock, Getty and some Envato items have "sensitive use" clauses). Check before using a stock photo **of people** in a campaign graphic. Prefer scenery and texture without identifiable people.
- No watermarked previews in deliverables.
- No real crowds from other events or other parties. Crowds come from the client's own event photos.
- No generated crowds, rallies or events presented as real.
- Party symbols and logos only from files the client supplies.

## ChatGPT plate prompts for vertical posters
```
Create an image: vertical 9:16 photorealistic <scene>, <light/time>, <mood>.
Keep the <upper-centre | lower third | left half> simple for a portrait and headline added later.
NO people, NO faces, NO crowds, NO text, NO letters, NO logos, NO flags.
```
Procedure (computer use): New chat (never reuse the user's chats), then prompt, wait about 60–90 s, right-click the image and choose **Download a copy** (or the viewer's download icon), then in Save As press Ctrl+A in the File name box and type a full path, then Save. Verify the file on disk.
Tested plates that worked: winding road through green hills at golden hour; a district government building with the national flag; a festival scene (decorated anthill with diyas); misty tribal hills at sunrise. Blur plates 3–8 px behind the subject, and check nothing behind the head reads as part of the person (e.g. garlands looking like flowers in the hair).
