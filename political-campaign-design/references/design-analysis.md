# Design analysis: what makes the references work

Six reference posters supplied by the user, plus the @ktrtrs reel-cover grammar and lessons from past jobs. Learn principles; never copy a layout, wording, logo or person.

---
## R1. "370 हटाना, पीएम मोदी का ऐतिहासिक फैसला": dramatic cinematic (9:16)
- **Language:** news-moment, heroic, fiery. Two zones: a flat dark-chocolate type panel (top 40%) and a fire-lit scene (bottom 60%), split by **one torn-paper edge** with a cream rim.
- **Hierarchy:** number, then name, then verdict. "370" and "मोदी" at 2–3× the other words; connectors (हटाना, पीएम, का) tucked into gaps (an interlocked lockup). The last line sits on a cream label bar.
- **Portrait:** chest-up cut-out at the bottom, cropped by the frame; angry and pointing at the viewer; stage mics in front anchor him to a real event. Hard clean mask.
- **Background:** orange radial glow plus **double-exposure ghosts** (roaring lion plus a larger faded image of the leader) at low opacity: symbolism, not scenery.
- **Light / grade:** monochrome warm; strong backlight around the head.
- **Type:** heavy Devanagari sans, peach fill with a solid deeper-orange offset (2.5D extrusion); one white word for contrast.
- **Finish:** a single torn edge, no clutter. Effective because one emotion is pushed to the maximum.

## R2. "अबकी बार बालेन सरकार": modern editorial typography (4:5)
- **Language:** magazine cover; white sky, giant red wordmark, power pose.
- **Composition:** symmetrical; the **wordmark sits behind the head** (subject overlaps the letters for depth); coverline blocks left and right; date top-right; credit strip at the bottom.
- **Portrait:** waist-up, arms crossed, sunglasses; clean cut-out, natural colour.
- **Background:** layered landscape (white sky, blue lake and mountains, a foreground of national flags crossing the torso) plus birds as motion accents.
- **Grade:** cool blue and white plus one accent red. **Type:** custom flat display, no effects; coverlines as bold line plus small body.
- Effective because type and portrait interlock; restraint makes the red pop.

## R3. "प्रामाणिक नेतृत्व, नव्या विकासाचा प्रवास": premium portrait (4:5)
- **Language:** warm, intimate, trustworthy; the **face is the hero** (large, smiling, filling about 60%).
- **Composition:** portrait bottom-left; condensed text stack right; ward number in a rounded badge; party logo small top-left.
- **Background:** a dark moody dusk city skyline, desaturated sepia, with dust/smoke particles.
- **Light:** soft front key; the background about 2 stops darker than the face. **Type:** condensed heavy Marathi, white with a **grunge texture fill**, stepped sizes.
- Effective because everything except the face is quiet.

## R4. "आम्ही नवीन नेता नाही तर नवा विचार": minimalist white editorial (4:5)
- **Language:** clean, airy, modern; white plus deep maroon plus gold-orange gradient words.
- **Composition:** full-body subject **walking** on a curved road graphic (journey metaphor); a giant pale name watermark behind; a huge white quotation-mark shape framing the quote; name plus bulleted credentials bottom-right.
- **Background:** a pencil-sketch skyline (illustration) plus soft smoke at the road base.
- **Type:** key words heavy with gradient fill, the rest regular grey; name heavy deep red.
- Effective because colour comes only from the subject and is echoed in the type.

## R5. "प्रणिताताई देवरे चिखलीकर": documentary photo collage (3:4)
- **Language:** proof of work. Central smiling cut-out in front of **polaroid cards** (school, bridge, water tap: real works), a faded crowd and a faded Zilla Parishad building behind; sky with birds.
- **Grade:** one golden-hour wash over everything, which unifies many photos into one image.
- **Type:** the name is the headline (gradient orange to red heavy Devanagari), location above, tagline below.
- Effective because the photos are evidence and the warm wash makes them a single story.

## R6. "भाग्य और जिम्मेदारियां बदलती रहती है…": statement plus rally (9:16)
- **Language:** dignified quote plus people's leader.
- **Composition:** a **torn cream scroll** (left/top) holds huge textured red words plus a small black line; a big smiling portrait right; a diagonal mic stand leads to the quote; a **real rally photo** strip at the bottom (the leader waving among supporters) fading up.
- **Background:** warm orange gradient, faint mandala ornament, sun flare behind the headline.
- **Type:** brick red with stone/grunge texture and a subtle bevel; flare across it.
- Effective because quote, face and real crowd tell three beats in one frame.

## @ktrtrs reel-cover grammar (studied frame-by-frame from 12 reels)
- Each cover tells **that reel's story** with a matching scene: a notebook on a desk (memories), drought next to a green river (irrigation), Parliament plus a map (delimitation), a classroom (education), rain plus a monument (a city's comeback).
- The leader is caught **mid-action** (speaking, pointing, fist, thinking) and colour-matched into the scene with rim light.
- **Lettering is the hero:** chunky textured Telugu/English, 3D, a script word on a brush slash, giant numbers (0, 10/10, 2.0). A different treatment per cover.
- Frame 0 of the reel **is** the poster; the grid reads like a wall of movie posters.

## Lessons from past jobs (avoid repeating these mistakes)
1. Flat gradient backgrounds plus a pasted cut-out looks templated. Use a story scene and integrate the light.
2. One template for every reel makes a dull grid. Rotate systems and vary layout and crop.
3. All black-and-white felt lifeless for a yellow-party (TDP) client. Keep brand colour dominant unless asked.
4. A dark drop shadow behind a cut-out shows as a halo on bright skies. Use a faint offset contact shadow.
5. Background garlands or branches behind the head read as flowers in the hair. Reframe or blur the plate.
6. Name strips and badges must not overlap the head. Place the subject after the overlays, and check.
7. Text centred by fixed coordinates drifts off brush strokes and ribbons. Measure the drawn shape and centre on it.
8. Photoshop dropped a letter around a ZWNJ (చైర్మన్‌గా rendered చైర్మనా). Zoom-check every word.
9. AI-generated full posters change the leader's face. Generate backgrounds only.
10. Remove Background on busy rally frames keeps neighbours and logos. Crop tight first, or use a cleaner (studio) photo.
