## Brand essentials (confirmed by the two SDOT reference reels; Format 4 below has its own approved frame, palette and fonts and takes priority for documentary explainers)
Black canvas (#090909) with rounded footage panels, the approved white serif SDOT wordmark with orange O (use the supplied asset, never rebuild), white text, pale translucent cards. ## Format 3: VO-led motion graphics

## SFX on every animation (standing rule from the user, Nov 2026)
Every on-screen animation gets its own sound effect, not just scene cuts. The user notices and asks for any that are missing, so cover all of them on the first pass:
- Label/pill pops: a soft UI pop on every pill, tag and label as it appears.
- Numbers: a run of ticks that rises in pitch and speed under every count-up (the whole time the number or bar is rising), then a landing click on the final value. Bars/tanks that fill get a filling sound (e.g. water filling plus a soft riser) under the rising count.
- Typed / typed-on words: keyboard typing for the length of each word.
- Lines that draw on (rivers on maps, timelines, strike-throughs, underlines): a pen or marker stroke for the length of the draw; timelines get a slide/whoosh, and the end pill gets a pop.
- Highlighter wipes: a marker-swipe sound.
- Paper or documents (source notes, clippings, crumpled paper, half-page splits): a paper slide as they enter and a paper drop when they land.
- Particles, dissolves, evaporation: a soft rising air hiss.
- Stamps: a real rubber-stamp hit (layer a soft impact on the key one).
- Turning points: a soft impact; distortion/liquify transitions: a whoosh.
Use real recordings (Openverse/Freesound CC0 or CC BY, credited in CREDITS.md); pick and trim them with earshot. Keep each SFX under the voice (peaks roughly -12 to -22 dBFS against VO at about -14 dBFS RMS), and check every cue's level in the SFX stem before delivering.
Music: neutral documentary underscore (soft cinematic pads/strings), steady, about 18-22 dB under the VO and ducked. Not eerie drones, not piano-led, no risers or sub-bass drops unless asked. Approved bed: "Ambient Documentary | Cinematic background" by tyops (CC BY 4.0, Freesound via Openverse), looped with an 8 s crossfade and gain-ridden so it never swells; gentle 4-6 dB dips at turning points only.
Approved SFX kit (all real, in the KrishnaWater project `public/sfx/`): UI Button Click (pops), Click_1 (number landings; also pitched up per tick for the rising count), Keyboard Typing (typed words), Underlining Stroke on Paper (strike-throughs, line draws), fast and slow marker strokes (highlighter), Sliding Envelope + Newspaper page drop (paper documents), stock water filling + Short Riser (filling tank), Soft Cinematic Impact (turning points), es-stamp (stamps), whoosh.soft / Whoosh_Simple (splits, timelines, smear). Rising count: ticks every ~45-90 ms that rise in pitch (x1 -> x1.9) and level (-30 -> -22 dBFS) across the whole time the value or bar is rising, then a click at -17 to -19 dBFS on the landing frame.
