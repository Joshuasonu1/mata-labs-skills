# Texture library

Keep a local library at `~/Documents/design-assets/textures/` (create it if missing) with a `LICENCES.md` listing the source of each file.

| Texture | Use | Blend | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Torn paper edge (white rim and fibres) | panel dividers, quote scrolls | Normal, with a soft shadow below | photograph or scan real torn paper; Freepik/Envato "torn paper png"; generate "macro photo of torn paper edge on transparent/white background" |
| Newsprint (grey column texture) | editorial panels | Multiply 20–40% | Unsplash/Pexels "newspaper texture"; scan |
| Kraft / cream paper | scroll panels (R6) | Normal plus Multiply grain | Pexels "paper texture" |
| Grunge / stone | textured headline fills (R3, R6) | Overlay/Multiply clipped to text | Unsplash "concrete texture", "stone texture" |
| Dust and smoke particles | atmosphere (R3) | Screen 30–60% | generate on black: "dust particles floating on pure black background" |
| Light leak / sun flare | warm highlight behind text (R6) | Screen 40–70% | generate on black; Pexels "light leak" |
| Fire / ember glow | cinematic backlight (R1) | Screen/Lighten | generate on black "orange fire glow, no objects" |
| Halftone dots | editorial print feel | Overlay 20–40% | Photoshop Filter > Pixelate > Color Halftone on a gradient |
| Mandala / ornament line art | cultural accent (R6) | Multiply/Overlay 8–15% | Freepik vectors (check licence) or simple self-drawn |
| Sketch skyline | minimalist background (R4) | Multiply 30–60% | Photoshop Filter Gallery > Sketch on a real skyline photo; or generate "pencil line sketch of <city> skyline on white" |
| Brush strokes (dry, bristle) | label bars, highlights | Normal, colour fill | Photoshop brushes (Kyle's), or scan real paint strokes |
| Film grain | final unifier | Overlay 25–35% | Filter > Noise > Add Noise on 50% grey |

## Quality bar
- Textures must be ≥ the export resolution (no upscaled paper).
- Torn edges need **three parts**: a fibrous white rim, a slight shadow, and a texture inside the paper. A plain jagged polygon looks fake.
- Never let a texture cover the face.
