# Reference analysis: six political posters

Six vertical posters supplied by the user (Hindi, Nepali, Marathi campaigns). They are **portrait-format posters**, not banners. Each section ends with how the idea translates to a wide cover. Learn the principles; never copy a layout, wording, logo or person.

---
## R1. "370 हटाना, पीएम मोदी का ऐतिहासिक फैसला": cinematic dramatic
- **Direction:** fiery, heroic, news-moment poster. Two-zone split: flat dark chocolate panel (top 40%) for type, fire-lit scene (bottom 60%) for the portrait.
- **Composition / hierarchy:** type stack reads number, then name, then verdict. "370" and "मोदी" are oversized (2–3× the other words); small connector words (हटाना, पीएम, का) tuck into the gaps of the big ones, giving a tight interlocked lockup. The last line sits on a cream label bar.
- **Portrait:** waist-up cut-out, bottom-centre, cropped by the frame edge. Mid-speech: angry expression, finger pointing at the viewer, two stage mics in front (props anchor him to an event). Hard clean mask.
- **Background:** orange-to-amber radial glow; **double-exposure ghosts** of a roaring lion and of the same leader (larger, lower opacity) behind him. Symbolism through ghosted imagery, not literal scenery.
- **Light / grade:** monochrome warm (brown, orange, yellow). Subject lit warm from behind (backlight glow around the head).
- **Type treatment:** heavy Devanagari sans in peach, with a solid offset shadow in deeper orange (2.5D extrusion). White used once for contrast (का).
- **Texture:** one torn-paper edge separating panel and scene, with a paper-white rim.
- **Cover translation:** split horizontally: type panel left (or right of avatar), fire glow plus ghost symbol behind portrait on the right. Keep the interlocked lockup and the one torn divider.

## R2. "अबकी बार बालेन सरकार": modern editorial
- **Direction:** magazine cover. White sky, giant red wordmark, subject in front of it.
- **Composition:** symmetrical. Subject centre, arms crossed, sunglasses, power pose. The **giant name wordmark sits behind the head** (subject overlaps the letters, which creates depth). Small text blocks balance left and right like magazine coverlines. Date top-right, platform tag top-left, credit strip bottom.
- **Background:** layered landscape: white sky, blue lake and mountains (middle), crowd of national flags in the foreground (bottom 20%) overlapping the subject's torso. Birds as tiny motion accents.
- **Grade:** cool blue plus white, with a single accent red (type and flags). Very restrained.
- **Type:** custom display Devanagari, red, flat (no effects). Mixed weights; supporting text in white on blue. Coverline style: short bold line plus small body.
- **Cover translation:** strongest banner idea: a wide wordmark across the full width behind the portrait, with coverlines on the side away from the avatar. Flags or crowd strip along the bottom edge (keep clear of the avatar circle).

## R3. "प्रामाणिक नेतृत्व, नव्या विकासाचा प्रवास": premium portrait
- **Direction:** warm, intimate, trustworthy. The face is the hero: big, smiling, close crop (head and shoulders filling 60% of the frame).
- **Composition:** portrait bottom-left, slightly off-centre, eye-line at upper third. Text stack top-right. Ward number in a rounded-square badge. Party logo small top-left.
- **Background:** dark moody night city skyline, desaturated brown, with dust/smoke particles drifting. The background is quiet so the face wins.
- **Light:** soft key on the face from front-left; background darker than the subject (luminance separation). Warm sepia grade, slight vignette.
- **Type:** condensed heavy Marathi display in white with a **distressed/grunge texture** inside the letters; the second line steps down in size; exclamation mark as accent.
- **Cover translation:** ideal for LinkedIn and X. Portrait large on the right third, dark atmospheric city or landscape, headline stack in the middle-right safe zone. Very few elements.

## R4. "आम्ही नवीन नेता नाही तर नवा विचार": minimalist white
- **Direction:** clean, airy, modern, women-leader editorial. Pure white or very light grey.
- **Composition:** full-body subject walking toward the camera on a **curved road graphic** (journey metaphor), left side. A giant pale watermark of her name fills the background. A huge white quotation-mark shape frames the quote on the right. Name and credentials bottom-right as a bulleted list.
- **Background:** pencil-sketch line drawing of a city skyline in light grey (illustration, not a photo), plus soft smoke at the road base.
- **Colour:** white plus maroon (her saree) plus orange-gold gradient words. Colour comes from the subject; the design echoes it.
- **Type:** mixed sizes inside the quote, with key words in gradient gold (नेता नाही, सत्ता नाही) and the rest dark grey. Name in deep red, heavy. Credentials small with bold labels.
- **Cover translation:** best LinkedIn system. White canvas, a sketch skyline relevant to the person's city or industry, full-body or 3/4 portrait walking, one quote, name and designation block. Lots of negative space.

## R5. "प्रणिताताई देवरे चिखलीकर": documentary photo collage
- **Direction:** track-record poster: "here's what I've done".
- **Composition:** central 3/4 cut-out (smiling, hands together) in front of **polaroid-style photo cards** (white borders, slight rotation, overlapping): a school, a bridge, a water tap. Each shows a delivered work. Behind them, a faded wide crowd and a faded Zilla Parishad building. Name in two-tone large type at the bottom; tagline beneath.
- **Background:** soft blue sky with clouds and birds, fading to warm haze at the bottom; everything washed with a cream-sepia glow so the many photos unify.
- **Light / grade:** golden-hour warm cast on every element (single unifying grade is what makes a collage look like one image).
- **Type:** the name is the headline (gradient orange-to-red heavy Devanagari); the circle and location above; the tagline below with ellipses. Credit tiny at the very bottom.
- **Cover translation:** wide banners are perfect for a horizontal strip of 3–5 real photo cards behind or beside the portrait. **Use only real photos of real works and events.**

## R6. "भाग्य और जिम्मेदारियां बदलती रहती है…": political leadership / statement
- **Direction:** dignified statement poster mixing quote typography and a rally photo.
- **Composition:** **torn cream paper scroll** on the left holds the quote (huge red textured words with small black connector lines). Large smiling portrait on the right, cut at the chest. A diagonal microphone on a stand leads the eye to the quote. The bottom 25% is a **real crowd photo** with the leader waving among supporters, fading up into the design.
- **Background:** warm orange gradient with a faint mandala or ornament texture (cultural cue) and a sun flare behind the text.
- **Type:** heavy Devanagari headline in brick red with **stone or grunge texture** and a subtle bevel; a lighter flare across it. Black medium-weight supporting line.
- **Cover translation:** quote panel (torn paper) on the left, beyond the avatar zone; portrait right; real rally strip along the bottom-right (avoid the bottom-left avatar area).

## What all six do well
1. **One hero, one message.** Never more than one dominant face and one dominant line.
2. **Luminance separation.** The subject is always brighter or more contrasty than the background directly behind the head.
3. **Unified grade.** Every layer shares the same colour cast (warm amber, cool blue, or sepia).
4. **Typography is designed, not typed:** interlocking sizes, at most two typefaces, effects that suit the mood (extrusion, grunge, gradient, flat).
5. **Props and symbols carry meaning:** mics, road, flags, works photos, lion ghost.
6. **Real photos for real claims** (crowds, works); illustration or generated plates only for mood.

## What to avoid (generic AI-poster tells)
Over-glow on everything, plastic skin, random sparkles or lens flares, five fonts, centred-everything symmetry with no hierarchy, fake crowds, unreadable text on busy backgrounds, logos redrawn by hand.
