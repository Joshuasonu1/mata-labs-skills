# Colour palettes and grading

Grade **everything together** at the end (top adjustment layers) so the plate, photos and portrait read as one photograph.

**Party / brand colours:** use the organisation's official hex values when supplied. Common Indian ones (approximate, confirm with the client): TDP yellow #FFD51E plus red #E2231A; BJP saffron #FF9933 plus green #138808; INC blue/saffron/green; BRS pink #E90878; YSRCP blue/green. Never mix rival parties' palettes.

## Grading stack (top of the PSD)
1. **Colour Lookup / Gradient Map** at 10–25% for the overall mood.
2. **Curves**: a soft S, lifted blacks for a print feel (cinematic) or clean blacks (minimal).
3. **Selective colour**: push the brand colour, pull competing hues.
4. **Vignette**: an elliptical darken (Multiply, 15–30%) toward the corners away from the text.
5. **Grain**: a 50% grey noise layer (2–3% mono) in Overlay, 25–35% opacity. It unifies photo and generated plate.

## Matching the portrait to the plate
- Sample the plate's brightest area next to the face, so the rim light uses that colour.
- Curves on the portrait: match its black point and white point to the plate's.
- Photo Filter (warming 85 or cooling 80) at 10–25%, clipped to the portrait.
- Check by desaturating the whole document. The subject must still separate by luminance.
