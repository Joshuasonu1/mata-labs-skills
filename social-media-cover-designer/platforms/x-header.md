# X (Twitter) header

## Official specs (checked Nov 2026)
- X Help "How to customize your profile" ([help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/how-to-customize-your-profile](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/how-to-customize-your-profile)): header ("banner") recommended **1500×500**; profile photo 400×400.
- X Help "Help with uploading a profile photo" ([help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/common-issues-when-uploading-profile-photo](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/common-issues-when-uploading-profile-photo)): displays vary, and **up to 60px top and bottom may be cropped** even at the recommended size. JPG/PNG/GIF, no animated GIF.

## Export preset
| File | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `*_x.jpg` | **1500×500** | sRGB JPG quality 90 (or PNG if it has flat colour or text, < 5 MB) |
Design at 3000×1000 and export down if the source photos allow.

## Safe zones (1500×500)
- **Vertical:** keep all text and faces within y **60–440**.
- **Avatar:** a circle overlapping the bottom-left, about x 20–260, y 310–500 on desktop (larger relative size on mobile). Keep it clear.
- **Mobile:** the header scales down to about 3:1 at phone width. Text under ~28px becomes unreadable, so use at most 2 text levels.
- Best placement: headline x 320–1000, face x 950–1480 (facing left into the text).

## Tips
- The header sits under the profile name and bio, so it's a mood piece. One bold line beats paragraphs.
- Editorial wordmark-behind-portrait works well at 3:1.
