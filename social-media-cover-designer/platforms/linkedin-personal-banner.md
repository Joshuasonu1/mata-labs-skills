# LinkedIn personal profile background

## Official specs (checked Nov 2026)
- LinkedIn Help "Add or change the background photo on your profile" ([linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217/add-or-change-the-background-photo-on-your-profile)): **1584×396** (4:1), JPG or PNG, under 8 MB. On desktop the headshot sits over the **left** of the background; on mobile it's **centred**.

## Safe zones (1584×396)
- **Desktop headshot:** a circle over the lower-left, about x 40–400, y 150–396. Keep it clear.
- **Mobile headshot:** **centred** at the bottom, about x 600–985, y 200–396. The lower-centre must also stay clear, which is why LinkedIn layouts put text and portrait on the **right third**, upper half.
- **Mobile side crop:** a few % each side. Keep 60px margins.
- Recommended: text block x 1000–1540, y 50–300; portrait (if used) x 1150–1584, or skip the portrait. The profile already shows the headshot, so a second face often feels redundant. Prefer the person **in context** (walking, speaking, at work) or no person at all.

## Tips
- The tone is professional: minimalist or premium portrait systems; political leadership only for politicians' own profiles.
- One line of value proposition plus a small designation, organisation or contact. Never repeat the name shown just below.
