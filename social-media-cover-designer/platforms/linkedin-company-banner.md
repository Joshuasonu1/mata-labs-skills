# LinkedIn company Page cover

## Official specs (checked Nov 2026)
- LinkedIn Help, Pages / landing-page specs table ([linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a417335](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a417335/content-and-image-specifications-for-landing-pages)) and the LinkedIn Learning "Your cover photo" lesson: **1128×191**, PNG or JPEG. File limit: guides say 8 MB, the official landing-page table says 6 MB, so **stay under 6 MB**.

## Safe zones (1128×191), ratio 5.9:1, very short
- **Logo:** a square logo overlaps the bottom-left, about x 20–200, y 100–191. Keep it clear.
- **Mobile:** crops sides and scales down; keep text in x 260–1060.
- Vertical: text within y 25–165.

## Layout rules
- **One line** of message (or brand plus a short tagline). Cap height 40–60px.
- Portrait optional and small (head and shoulders, height ≤ 191px, right edge), or an environment image (office, city, constituency).
- Documentary collage works as a thin strip of 4–6 photo tiles behind a gradient.
