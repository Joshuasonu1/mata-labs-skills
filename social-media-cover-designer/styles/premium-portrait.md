# Style 3: Premium portrait (from R3)

**Mood:** warm, trustworthy, personal. The face is the hero. For personal branding, introductions, "your representative".

## Composition
- A **large** head-and-shoulders portrait (60–75% of the canvas height for the face plus shoulders), on the right third, eye-line at about 35% from the top.
- Text stack on the opposite side (respecting the avatar): two-tier condensed headline plus small designation.
- A badge element (constituency or ward number, years of service) in a rounded rectangle, used sparingly.
- Logo small in a top corner (client-supplied file only).

## Background
Dark, moody, quiet: a dusk city skyline, a soft-focus office, or a constituency landmark at night, desaturated with warm sepia. Floating dust or smoke particles (Screen 30–50%).
ChatGPT prompt: *"wide 4:1 moody dusk city skyline, warm brown haze, soft bokeh lights, dark and desaturated, empty right half sky, no people, no text"*.

## Lighting
Soft front key on the face; the background is **two stops darker** than the face. A gentle warm rim on the shadow side.

## Typography
Condensed heavy display, white with a clipped grunge texture (Overlay 50%); the second tier smaller; one accent mark (!) or a thin rule.

## Finish
Sepia gradient map 15%; vignette 30%; grain 30%.

## Watch
The portrait must be high-res (no upscaling beyond 1.5×) because faces are big here.
