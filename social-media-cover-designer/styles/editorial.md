# Style 2: Modern editorial (from R2)

**Mood:** confident, modern, magazine-cover. For campaign slogans, "vote for", issue framing, youth appeal.

## Composition
- A **giant wordmark** (name or slogan) spans 70–100% of the width **behind the subject's head**. The cut-out overlaps the letters (put the type layer below the portrait).
- Subject in a power pose (arms crossed, hands on hips), centred or right of centre.
- Coverlines: 1–2 small text blocks on the free side (bold line plus small body), aligned to a grid.
- A foreground band (flags, crowd heads, landscape edge) crosses the lower torso.
- Small meta (date, handle, constituency) in the top corners, away from the avatar.

## Background
Layered landscape: clean sky top (white to pale blue), a horizon band (lake, hills, city), foreground band. ChatGPT prompt: *"wide 3:1 photoreal landscape, pale bright sky top half, calm blue lake and distant hills across the middle, nothing in the centre, no people, no text"*.

## Colour
Cool, clean whites and blues; one accent colour for the wordmark and flags (brand or party).

## Typography
Flat display type (no effects), heavy weight, tight leading; wordmark cap height 45–60% of the canvas. Coverlines in a contrasting text weight.

## Finish
Light grain, minimal vignette, crisp edges. Birds or flags as small motion accents (real or generated on transparent, sparing).

## Platform note
Excellent for X and Facebook. For LinkedIn personal, push the subject right; the wordmark must clear the centred mobile avatar.
