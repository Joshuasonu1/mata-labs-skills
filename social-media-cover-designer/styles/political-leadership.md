# Style 6: Bold political leadership (from R6, plus mass-rally energy)

**Mood:** dignified strength, people's leader. For statements, quotes, rally campaigns, party identity.

## Composition
- A **torn cream paper scroll** panel on the message side holding the quote or slogan (huge textured headline plus a small connector line).
- A large portrait (smiling or speaking) on the hero side, cut at the chest.
- A diagonal prop (microphone on a stand, flag pole) leading from the portrait toward the text.
- **A real rally strip** along the bottom (the leader among supporters or crowds from the client's events) fading up into the design, kept off the avatar corner.
- Optional cultural ornament (mandala, rangoli line, temple silhouette) at 8–15%.

## Background
Party-colour gradient (e.g. saffron to deep orange, TDP yellow to warm red, or the brand) plus a sun flare behind the headline plus an ornament texture.
ChatGPT prompt: *"wide 3:1 warm golden-orange abstract background with soft sun flare at the left, faint traditional mandala pattern, no people, no text"*.

## Lighting
Warm sunlight from the text side; rim on the portrait's text-facing edge; the crowd strip slightly darker and warmer.

## Typography
Headline in a heavy display in brick red or party colour with a clipped stone or grunge texture and a subtle bevel (size 3, soft); a light flare crossing the headline (Screen). Connector line in black medium. Name and designation small near the portrait.

## Finish
Torn scroll with a fibrous edge and a curl shadow; grain 30%; vignette on the far corner.

## Rules
Crowds are real and the client's own. Party symbols only from supplied files. Keep rival colours out.
