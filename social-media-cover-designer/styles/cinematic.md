# Style 1: Cinematic dramatic (from R1)

**Mood:** decisive, heroic, "historic moment". For milestones, decisions, victories, strong statements.

## Composition
- Two zones divided by **one** torn-paper edge (vertical diagonal on wide covers): a flat dark type panel on the message side, and a fire-lit scene on the hero side.
- Portrait chest-up, mid-gesture (speaking, pointing, fist), cropped by the bottom edge. Props such as stage mics or a podium edge in front of the torso.
- A **ghost layer** behind the portrait: a symbolic double exposure (lion, monument, constituency landmark, a larger faded image of the same leader) at 15–35% opacity, Screen or Lighten.

## Background
- Flat panel: #2B1509 to #1A0D06 with subtle paper grain.
- Scene: a radial glow (orange #FF8A1E core to dark edges), plus ember or fire light textures (generated on black, Screen), plus haze.
- ChatGPT prompt: *"wide 3:1 cinematic dark background, intense orange-golden fire glow and embers rising from the bottom right, smoky atmosphere, no people, no text, no objects, dark left third for typography"*.

## Lighting
Backlight from behind the subject (warm rim on hair and shoulders), key from the front-left, low-key overall. Rim light: Outer Glow or painted soft light on the edge facing the glow, orange, 20–30%.

## Typography
- Interlocked lockup: 1–2 hero words (number or name) at 2–3× size, connectors small beside them.
- Peach/cream #FFD8B0 fill with a solid 2.5D extrusion (Drop Shadow, Normal 100%, distance 10px, size 0, #C2541A); one word in white for contrast.
- The last line on a cream label bar with dark text.

## Texture and finish
Torn edge with a paper rim; grain 30%; vignette 25%; Curves with lifted blacks.

## Do / Don't
Do keep the face the brightest thing. Don't put fire behind the face so it competes, and don't over-glow (no Outer Glow > 30% on the subject).
