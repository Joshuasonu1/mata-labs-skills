# Style 5: Documentary photo collage (from R5)

**Mood:** proof of work, warm, grounded. For track records, development works, schemes, constituency service.

## Composition
- Central or right-side 3/4 cut-out (smiling, approachable).
- **3–5 photo cards** (white border 2–3% of the card, rotation −6° to +6°, soft drop shadow, slight overlap) showing **real** works and events: schools, roads, water, hospitals, meetings.
- Behind: a faded wide crowd and a faded landmark (office, district building) at 20–40% opacity, blended into the sky.
- Name as the headline at the bottom or side (gradient fill), with location above and tagline below.

## Background
Sky gradient (blue to cream haze) plus birds, unified by a golden-hour wash. A generated sky is fine; **every photo card and crowd must be the client's real photo**.
ChatGPT prompt: *"wide 3:1 soft blue sky with light clouds fading to warm cream haze at the bottom, golden hour, a few distant birds, no people, no text, no buildings"*.

## Colour and grade
Photo Filter (warming, 30%) over the **whole** document so the photos match; Curves lift; slight bloom on the highlights.

## Typography
Name: heavy display with a 2-stop gradient (orange to deep red, or brand); location small above; tagline in italic or regular.

## Finish
Card shadows consistent with one light direction; grain 25%; credit or handle tiny.

## Rules
Cards must be legible at mobile size: on banners use ≤ 4 cards, each ≥ 20% of the canvas height. Never use stock or generated photos as "works done".
