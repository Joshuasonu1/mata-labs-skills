# Style 4: Minimalist professional (from R4)

**Mood:** clean, modern, thoughtful. The default for LinkedIn, professionals, women leaders, policy and thought leadership.

## Composition
- White or warm-grey canvas, ≥ 40% negative space.
- The subject full-body or 3/4, **in motion** (walking, gesturing) on a simple graphic path (a curved road ribbon or a line) that leads toward the text.
- A giant pale **watermark word** (name or theme) at 5–8% opacity behind everything.
- A quote panel: an oversized quotation-mark shape (white with a soft shadow) holding a 3–5 line quote with key words coloured.
- Name and designation block plus 2–4 credentials with bold labels.

## Background
Pencil-sketch line art of a skyline or landmark (Multiply 30–50%), plus soft white smoke at the base.
ChatGPT prompt: *"wide 4:1 minimal pencil line sketch of a modern Indian city skyline on pure white, light grey lines, lots of empty white space, no people, no text"*.

## Colour
Neutral; colour comes from the subject's clothing. Echo it in the key words (gradient gold-orange or the brand deep red).

## Typography
Mixed weights inside the quote (key phrases heavy plus gradient, the rest regular dark grey); name in deep brand colour, heavy; credentials small.

## Finish
No vignette, very light grain (10–15%), a soft contact shadow under the feet or path.

## LinkedIn placement
Subject right third walking left; quote centre-right; the lower-centre stays clear for the mobile avatar.
